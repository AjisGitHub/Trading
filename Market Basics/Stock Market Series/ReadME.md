# Stock Market Series

👋 Hi guys!

I have decided to start a Stock Market & Investing Education Series where we’ll learn about the stock market from absolute basics to advanced concepts.

The purpose of this series is to make stock market education simple, practical, and easy to understand, especially for beginners.

We’ll not just learn definitions. We’ll understand how things actually work, how to analyze investments, how to manage risk, and how to make better financial decisions.
